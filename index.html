PINBALL

Three small physics & puzzle games

Vault Bumper

Rack up points, crack the vault. A numbered-bumper pinball board — hit a bumper for its number × 100, reach the target score to pop the vault door.

Turnkey

Unlock every keyhole. A Lights Out–style puzzle where each click toggles a keyhole and its orthogonal neighbors.

Keyfall

A gravity keyhole puzzle — rotate a pivot arm and let physics carry the ball into place.
