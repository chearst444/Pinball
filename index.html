KEYFALL

A gravity keyhole puzzle

Drop the ball, then click the magenta arms to steer it.
Attempt 1

How to play

  • Teal platforms and ledges are fixed — they just shape the fall.
  • Magenta pivot arms rotate 90° on click or tap, redirecting the ball.
  • Yellow numbered keyholes must all be triggered in one continuous drop.
  • Clear every keyhole before the ball exits the bottom to unlock the bonus star.
