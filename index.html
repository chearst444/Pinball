How to play
  • Flippers: tap the on-screen buttons, tap the left/right side of the table, or use the arrow keys (or Z / M).
  • Press Space or Launch Ball to drop a new ball onto the table.
  • Magenta bumpers pop the ball away and score points; teal pegs give it a smaller nudge.
  • Two rows of side paddles fire along with the flipper on their side — they don’t score, but they’ll help keep the ball bouncing.
  • Teal rails guide the ball around the table.
  • Miss with both flippers and the ball drains down the middle — you get 3 balls per game.
